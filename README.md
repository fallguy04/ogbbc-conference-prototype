# Annual Conference prototype

An early prototype of the Annual Conference tools for ogbbc.org, built as a WordPress plugin. It is for the webmasters and the Committee of Arrangements to look at and talk over.

- **Everyone in it is made up.** Every person, household, phone number and count is a sample. It is not the real site, and nothing in it is real data.
- **It is early.** Many details are still being decided. What you see is a rough draft of how things could work, not a decision.

## Open it

https://playground.wordpress.net/?mode=seamless&blueprint-url=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fallguy04/ogbbc-conference-prototype/main/blueprint.json

The link runs the whole site inside your own web browser, through WordPress Playground (a tool from WordPress.org). Nothing is installed on your computer, what you do stays in your browser, and it starts fresh each time you open the link. The first visit can take a minute to load.

## The tour

A small card in the corner walks through nine scenes, one at a time: the Conference page, registering, Your sign-ups, volunteering, the guide on a phone during Conference, the printed schedule, the Committee of Arrangements' home, Lodging's rooms, and the webmasters' view. Each scene signs in the right made-up person and shows the date that fills the screen. Next and Back move between them; **Scenes** lists them all and lets you see a page as someone else. The card can be closed and opened again.

No email is ever sent from the prototype.

## What's in this folder

- `blueprint.json`: what WordPress Playground sets up (the plugin, a stand-in for the site's theme, and the sample data).
- `ogc-conference.zip`: the plugin.
- `ogbbc-standin.zip`: a stand-in for the ogbbc.org theme's header and footer.
